Getting started

Photostead has two parts: the Photostead server, which keeps your library on hardware you own (a home server, NAS or computer), and Photostead for iPhone, which backs up your iPhone to it and lets you browse everything. The app needs a Photostead server to pair with.

Getting a server: the install guide covers a home server or NAS (Unraid or Docker) and a Mac or Windows PC. Once it’s running:

  1. On your computer, open Photostead in a web browser and sign in.
  2. Open Devices & remote access and choose Show a pairing code.
  3. On your iPhone, point the Camera at the QR code, tap the banner that appears, and confirm in Photostead. You can also type the code into the app.
  4. Allow access to your photos. Backing up starts right away, newest first.

Want to look around first? The live demo runs Photostead in your browser on a sample library.

Questions and answers

Do I need my own server?

Yes. Photostead keeps your library on hardware you control instead of in someone else’s cloud, so the app pairs with a Photostead server run by you or someone in your household. If someone in your home already runs one, ask them for a pairing code. If not, the install guide walks you through setting one up on a home server, NAS, Mac or Windows PC.

How do I back up when I’m away from home?

At home, the app reaches your server on your home network. Away from home, it gets in through your server’s secure address once whoever runs the server turns on remote access (Devices & remote access → Tailscale, with Funnel on): pair the phone once at home, and from then on it works from anywhere with nothing else to install. With Funnel off, install the Tailscale app on your iPhone and sign in to the same tailnet as your server instead. The app tries each of your server’s addresses in turn and uses the one that answers.

Why hasn’t my backup finished?

The pill under the Library’s title shows what’s happening. Some things to check:

  • Wi-Fi: backups use Wi-Fi only, unless you turn on Use cellular data in the app’s settings.
  • Background time: iOS decides when apps may work in the background, usually overnight while the phone is charging. Opening the app speeds things up.
  • Swiping the app away in the app switcher stops transfers that are under way. They pick up again the next time you open the app.
  • Photos access: with limited access, only the photos you chose are backed up. To back up everything, allow full access in Settings → Photostead → Photos.
  • iCloud Photos: originals that are only in iCloud are downloaded first, which takes longer on a slow connection.
Does Photostead change or delete anything on my iPhone?

No. The app reads your originals and sends a copy to your server. It never edits or deletes anything in your iPhone’s Photos library, and it adds to it only when you save a photo or video from Photostead to Photos. It backs up each item’s original (for a Live Photo, both the photo and its motion), not edits made on the phone.

Can I bring in photos from Google Photos or Apple Photos?

Yes. From Google Photos, export your library at takeout.google.com, unzip it, and drop the Takeout folder onto Photostead in your web browser. From Photos on a Mac, choose File › Export › Export Unmodified Originals with Export IPTC as XMP ticked, then drop the folder in. Google’s export brings dates, descriptions, places, favorites and albums; Apple’s brings captions, keywords, places and dates. Anything already in your library is kept only once. Import in Photostead’s sidebar shows every way in.

How do I turn off backups, or unpair the app?

Tap your picture at the top right, then turn off Back up this iPhone, or choose Unpair this phone to disconnect the app from your server. Unpairing doesn’t delete anything from your Photostead library or from your iPhone. To back up again, pair again with a new code.

How do I delete my data?

Your library lives on your own Photostead server, not with us, so it’s always in your control. To delete photos, move them to the trash in Photostead and empty it. To disconnect the app, unpair it, then delete it to remove what it keeps on your iPhone (unpair first, so the pairing token goes too). Removing a person’s whole account from a server isn’t in Photostead yet; write to us if you need it. We don’t hold any of your data; if you’ve emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, just ask. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices does the app support?

Photostead for iPhone needs iOS 17 or later. Photostead on the web works in current versions of Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Is my library private?

Yes. Your photos go only to your own server, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking. Face recognition, search, Ask and place names all work on your server; only the Places map’s background comes from a map service. Read more in the Privacy Policy.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Email hello@photostead.com. For a problem, it helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version, what you were doing, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots are welcome.
